How this was calculated

Road safety comes first

A better route saves time by avoiding congestion. It never means driving faster. Route suggestions help you plan; they do not prevent accidents, and road signs, signals and traffic police instructions always come first.

Keep to the speed limit

Follow the posted limit for your road and vehicle. Slow down further near schools, hospitals and markets, in rain and at night.

Leave a safe gap

Keep at least three seconds behind the vehicle ahead: when it passes a fixed point, count three seconds before you reach it. Double the gap in rain or poor light.

Obey traffic signals

Red means stop behind the stop line. Amber means stop if you safely can, not speed up. Give way to pedestrians at crossings.

Drive responsibly

Helmets for every two-wheeler rider and pillion, seat belts for everyone in a car. No phone while driving, no drink-driving, and no riding against traffic or on footpaths to take a shortcut.

About this prototype

A Community Service Project software prototype that shows how comparing routes by journey time, distance and traffic can support better travel decisions.

Real map mode

  • Roads, one-way rules, speed limits, hospitals, schools, markets and place names come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL).
  • Traffic is estimated from the time of day and the type of road. It is not live traffic data.
  • Safety hazards are worked out from the map (school zones, markets, highways, busy junctions). Accident black spots come from the list in js/blackspots.js.
  • Map data can have gaps. Always follow road signs, signals and the traffic police.

Demo town mode

  • The town, its 12 locations and 19 roads are a sample network, not real streets.
  • Road lengths, free-flow speeds and traffic levels are fixed demo values.
  • Road hazards (black spots, school zones, markets, lighting, footpaths) and their risk weights are sample values, not accident records.
  • Journey times are estimates and ignore signals, turns and stops.
  • No live traffic, GPS or map service is used, so the demo works offline.
